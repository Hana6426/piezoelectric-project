Quartz e11 — Unrelaxed Berry-phase finite difference (±0.2%)

P(0%) = -0.0778609 C/m²
P(+0.2%) = +0.3962552 C/m²
P(-0.2%) = -0.1182087 C/m²

Central difference:
e11 ≈ +128.60 C/m²

Forward difference:
e11 ≈ +237.06 C/m²

Notes:

Still wildly unphysical compared to the expected quartz e₁₁ ≈ +0.17 C/m².

Even small ±0.2% strain produces large nonlinear jumps in Berry-phase polarization.

Indicates branch switching and severe nonlinearity in polarization vs. strain.

Confirms that simple finite-difference Berry-phase is not reliable for quartz.

For weak piezoelectrics like quartz, DFPT or ultra-small strains (<0.05%) with explicit branch tracking is required.
