Quartz e11 — small-strain Berry finite difference (±0.05%, clamped ions)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

P(0)         = -0.0778609  C/m^2
P(-0.05%)    = -0.0939648  C/m^2
P(+0.05%)    = -0.4012326  C/m^2

Central finite-difference estimate:
e11 ≈ [P(+ε) - P(-ε)] / (2ε)  with ε = 0.0005

e11 ≈ -307.27 C/m^2

Notes:
- Result is unphysically large (expected |e11| ~ 0.1–0.2 C/m^2 from DFPT/experiment).
- Structure is clamped (no internal relaxation under strain).
- Even at ±0.05% strain, Berry-phase polarization appears to jump branches or exhibit strong nonlinearity.
- Together with our ±0.2% and ±0.5% tests, this suggests that within our QE build/settings,
  finite-difference Berry calculations for quartz e11 are not numerically robust.
